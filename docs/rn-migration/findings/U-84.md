### 4. The fix

`DiaryDayModel` publishes in its snapshot everything the day sheet draws: `heading`, `isToday`, `eatenKcal`, `goalKcal`,
`leftKcal`, `burnedKcal`, and `kcal` on each section next to its items. `publishDiaryDay(data, {heading, isToday})` builds it;
`refresh()` replaces the snapshot on every change, never mutates it; the heading and the today flag belong to the day the rows
were read for. `DiaryDayView` keeps its subscription and reads `snapshot.*`. The getters of the model stay (they read the
snapshot). No `useMemo`, no lint suppression. The test doubles of `DiaryDayView.test.tsx` and `DiaryDayViewUndoQueue.test.tsx`
build their snapshot through `publishDiaryDay`; no test sentence changed.
