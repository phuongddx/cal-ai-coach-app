## Pitfalls met in U-14

1. **Path arithmetic.** `scripts/rn/evidence/lib.sh` sits three levels under the repo root —
   `scripts/rn/e2e-sync-app.sh` needed two `..`, the evidence lib needs three. The wrong root
   failed silently: Metro "started" into a `cd` error and a transcript was written to a stray
   `scripts/docs/…` outside the repo (deleted). Compute roots from `BASH_SOURCE` and test with a
   subset run before trusting a new lib.
2. **Killing the recorded Metro pid does not always free 8081.** `npx expo start` wraps the expo
   process; killing the npx pid can orphan the listener. `metro_stop` kills the recorded pid,
   then — only if the port is still held — kills exactly the 8081 listener (`lsof -ti :8081`),
   and verifies the port is free. Never a blanket kill of node processes.
3. **A fatal abort can leak Metro.** A standalone script that starts Metro and then exits early
   (FATAL) left Metro serving 8081; the next `metro_start` refuses to run. `metro_start` now
   installs an `EXIT` trap that calls `metro_stop`, so whoever started Metro always stops it.
4. **`app_open` from the home screen can fail silently.** After the app was backgrounded, the
   first open produced no bundle request (nothing in the Metro log) and no title within 90 s.
   `app_open` now retries the dev-client URL (45 s on the first attempt, the requested timeout on
   the second) and `dismiss_open_dialog` taps Open for up to 10 s. Every flow starts with
   `app_open`, so each flow is independent of the simulator's current state.
5. **Titles move during the tab transition.** For ~1 s after a tab tap the incoming title is
   reported at an intermediate y (< 200) before it settles (placeholder titles settle at y≈408).
   Matching "StaticText with y < 700" alone can catch the transient frame; and
   `accessibilityRole="header"` renders as type `Heading`, not StaticText. `_wait_title` accepts
   tab-name titles only once settled (y > 200) and Heading/non-tab titles anywhere < 700.
6. **StaticTexts are double-reported.** Every text element appears twice in the describe-ui tree
   with identical frames. Harmless for matching; first-match functions may cite either copy.
7. **Covered rows are still in the tree.** The Today meal list extends under the glass tab bar —
   the last seeded row ("Chicken rice bowl") is partly covered by the bar and the Scan button.
   `wait_for_text` finds covered text, but a flow that must TAP a covered row needs a swipe first
   (flow 03 has the swipe fallback; it never fired because the text was found without it).
8. **Spike pitfalls re-confirmed on main:** the deep-link confirmation dialog appears
   inconsistently (handled by the bounded Open taps — seen on the seed link, absent elsewhere);
   Metro started with `CI=1` never reloads (never used here; `expo start` runs without CI).
9. **Spike pitfall 15 is outdated for the tab bar** (see the table above): label taps work on the
   glass tab bar on main. It was NOT re-verified for the Health sheet or other glass surfaces.
