## 2. Findings

1. **No analyzing checklist, no dwell timer.** The Ember analyzing frame has no step list. The model no longer plays
   steps with a 0.5 s dwell or a 5 s watchdog: a response lands in review the moment the call settles. `AnalyzingStep`
   is removed. Swift's dwell and fourth step are dropped on purpose.
2. **`captureCorrection` still clears "Review needed"** (as in Swift). The owner wrote "reporting does not change the
   diary entry". That holds for the entry, but a report also lifts the Save gate on an unresolved item. If a report
   should not lift the gate, the user must remove the item or fix it first. **Decision for the owner.**
3. **Report a problem sends nothing.** The contract has no report endpoint. (Corrected in U-72b part two, section H: the
   photo toggle is gone, the dock reads "Save report" and the sent state says the report is saved on this device.)
4. **Per-item entry point.** "Report a problem" is a link on each item card (`scan.fixIssue.<id>`), not a dock note
   row. The sheet has item pills to retarget, direct fixes first (Wrong amount opens the grams editor, Shouldn't be
   here removes the item), then the note.
5. **The Saved screen replaces the toast and the peek.** Undo, Scan another and View diary live on it; ED-Safe shows the
   dish without kcal and drops the Today card.
6. **Warning chip replaced** by a per-item "includes hidden fat" line (`scan.hiddenFat.<id>`).
7. **Model additions.** `mealMacros()` (whole grams from current grams), an `isUndoing` guard so a second undo while
   one runs tombstones each entry once, `captureCorrection(kind, note, itemId)`,
   `removeItem`. Displayed kcal is still recomputed from current grams (I6).

## A. The merge and `GlassIconButton` (items 1 and 2)

`origin/main` merged (merge commit `7c44fb8`). The only conflict was `GlassIconButton.tsx` and its test. Resolved so
that all three things are present: `disabled` with `glassDisabledGlyphAlpha = 0.4` (main), the native glass and
`hasNativeGlass()` (branch), and `accessibilityValueText` with its test.

**How the disabled glass looks:** the glass (native `GlassView` on iOS 26, the translucent disc below) is drawn at full
strength and keeps its hairline; only the glyph goes to 40% of its ink. The button ignores presses, reports
`accessibilityState.disabled` and does not dim on press. A disabled button therefore stays a visible disc with a quiet
glyph on every system. Main's `opacity: pressed ? 0.7 : 1` is kept for enabled buttons only.

Changed expectation (not a sentence): the iOS 18 fallback test expected `withAlpha(darkColors.bg, 0.45)`; main's round-3
token is `darkColors.mediaScrim`, so it now expects that. New test: "a disabled button keeps its glass and its disc,
ignores presses and reports the state". The duplicate trailing "spoken value" describe left by the merge is removed.

`git diff --name-only origin/main...HEAD -- apps/mobile/src/design` prints only `GlassIconButton.tsx` and
`GlassIconButton.test.tsx`.
