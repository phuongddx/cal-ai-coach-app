## 1. Design changes

- **Header.** The date is the subtitle ("Saturday, 12 Sep") over the title "Today"; the right side holds the calendar
  glass button (`GlassIconButton`, icon `cal`, label "Open diary", `today.openDiary`). `today.dateSelector` is on the
  date subtitle. **What the date selector did before:** it was a control. A press toggled the visibility of the week
  strip (state in `TodayView`), and it drew a chevron. It keeps that toggle and gains the hint "Shows or hides the
  week strip"; the chevron is gone because the frame has none. The diary entry point it never had moves to the glass button.
- **Week strip.** Seven cells of 44 by 56 pt, radius `radius.md` (14), the weekday initial over a 17 pt rounded day
  number. The selected day takes `accentTint` fill and `accentInk` text; the others `text3` initial and `text` number.
  The row spreads over the width (`flexGrow: 1`, `space-between`) and scrolls only on a narrow screen.
- **Ember card.** `EmberCard` holds `CalorieRing variant="md" onEmber` (150 pt, 13 pt stroke, `size.ringMd` and
  `size.ringMdStroke`) with the centre "kcal left" and, on the right, "Eaten" and "Goal" (grouped numbers, 20 pt
  rounded bold on `emberText`, labels on `emberText2`). `today.ring` stays on the wrapper of the card content.
- **`CalorieRing` (design, additive).** `RingVariant` gains `'md'`. New optional props `centerLabel` (default
  "remaining", so no existing caller changes), `aside` and `edSafeAside` (the node drawn to the right of the ring in
  the normal mode and under ED-Safe; the DS chooses, so the feature holds no branch: rule 20, invariant I7). The `md`
  number uses `textStyles.numLg` (36/36) with `fitWidth`; "On track" is 22 pt bold and the day caption 13 pt medium.
  Without `aside` the component returns the bare ring as before. Tests added in `CalorieRing.test.tsx` (7 new, 17 in
  the file). The a11y label sentences of the ring are unchanged.
- **Macro grid.** One card, two rows of two cells: Protein, Carbs / Fat, Fiber. Each cell: label (one line), value
  "82 / 130 g" (one line, shrinks to fit) and the DS `MacroBar`. It replaces the row of three mini cards and the
  separate fiber card, so the third card is no longer cut at the right edge (the U-79 screenshot of `main`).
- **Water.** `WaterCard` and `WaterLogSheet` are mounted as they are (U-74 restyles them). `today.waterCard` kept; the
  card is mounted in both modes.
- **Streak.** A card with the `accentTint` tile holding the flame icon in `accentInk`, "12-day streak" and the
  freeze line "1 freeze available". Always mounted, in every mode (as before); the DS `StreakCard` is no longer used by Today.
- **Meals.** Head "Meals" with a "Diary" link (`today.mealsDiary`); the rows (`FoodRow`, badge, kcal) sit in one card;
  a day without meals says "No meals logged yet". The bottom padding and `TODAY_BOTTOM_INSET` are unchanged: the
  last meal clears the tab bar and the Scan button.
- **Offline (frame 08).** A caution chip "Offline · syncs later" in the header (`cautionTint` fill, `cautionInk`
  text, 32 pt) with `offline.badge`. The info banner is gone. A meal that waits for the sync carries
  "· syncs later" in its meta line. The dark look is the token swap (`bg` true black, `card` warm dark): no colour
  is written in the feature.
- **ED-Safe (frame 09).** The ring centre is the DS "On track" with the day fraction. The right column is the
  guidance sentence "Steady meals keep energy even. {Meal} is next." with the second sentence on `emberText`; the
  meal comes from the clock (`nextMealName`: before 11 Breakfast, before 15 Lunch, before 21 Dinner, then
  Breakfast). The macro grid, the water card, the streak and the badges stay; the meal rows drop the kcal through
  `FoodRow`; no red "over" state is drawn. `today.eaten` and `today.goalKcal` stay as empty wrappers.
- **Scan button.** Accent circle at `size.fab` (62) with the camera icon in `onAccent` (was a lime circle with a
  plus). Same position and bottom offset.
- **Tab bar.** Coach `sparkles` (Android `auto_awesome`), Profile `person.crop.circle` (`account_circle`); Today
  `sun.max` and Progress `chart.line.uptrend.xyaxis` are kept (the canvas names `#i-sun` and `#i-chart`, which the
  SF names already express).
- **Route.** `app/(tabs)/index.tsx` no longer passes `onOpenHealthSettings` and no longer imports `Linking`.

## 2. Deviations

1. **No "N of M meals" in the ring (frame 09: "2 of 3 meals"; brief: left out if the model has no source).** The model
   holds the meals logged but no number of meals the plan expects, so the line is not drawn; the ring shows "On
   track" and the day fraction ("50% of day"), as the DS already did. Open question 1.
2. **"Burned" is not drawn** (the brief): it needs the Apple Health steps.
3. **Meal row (`FoodRow`, a DS component I may not change).** The kcal reads "624 kcal" with a chevron, the frame
   "624" alone; the leading tile is the fork-and-knife icon, not a photo thumb; the "syncs later" suffix is added to the
   meta line, and `FoodRow` also draws its own "Syncs later" chip next to it only if `syncPending` is passed:
   Today does not pass it, so the text appears once. Open question 2.
4. **The offline chip does not replace the calendar button.** Frame 08 shows the chip instead of the button. Here the
   chip sits left of the button, so `today.openDiary` (the only calendar entry point) stays reachable offline. A one-line change if you want the frame literally.
5. **Borders and glass.** Cards carry a hairline `border` token on `card`; I did not compare the edge treatment with the frames pixel by pixel (stage B). The glass button has no blur (U-71b: no blur package).
6. **Times use AM/PM** ("Breakfast · 9:00 AM") as the app already does; the frames write 24 h ("8:05").
7. **"No streak yet"** is shown at a streak of 0 (the frame has no zero state), with "No freezes available" when none are left.
8. **The next meal is a clock fact**, not a plan fact (the hour edges of `mealSlotFor`). At night (21:00 and later) it says
   "Breakfast is next." The copy is the frame's; the source is the clock.
9. **Meal times and badge copy** (the "High" / "Medium" mini badge) come from the existing `ConfidenceBadge compact`; its text is "Low — review needed" etc., not the single word of the frame.
10. **Macro goals** show what the target says (the seed: Carbs 220), not the frame's sample (210).

## 5. The four-digit number and the macro grid (argued here, proven in stage B)

Jest cannot measure text, so these are arguments, not proofs.

- **Four digits in the 150 pt ring.** The ring's inner width is 150 − 2 × (13 stroke + `space.sm`) ≈ 108 pt. "2150" at
  36 pt bold rounded with tabular numerals is about 4 × 21.6 = 86 pt wide, which fits; at a larger text size the
  `ScaledNumber` carries `fitWidth` (`numberOfLines=1`, `adjustsFontSizeToFit`, `minimumFontScale 0.5`), so it shrinks
  instead of wrapping (the "1607" → "16 / 07" bug of the 184 pt hero ring). The test asserts those props reach the text.
  Stage B: a day with nothing eaten and a four-digit goal, in light and dark.
- **Macro grid.** Each cell is `flex: 1, minWidth: 0` in a row of two, so a cell can never be wider than half the
  card and the grid cannot overflow the screen. Labels use `numberOfLines=1` and are not shrinkable; values use
  `numberOfLines=1`, `adjustsFontSizeToFit` and `flexShrink`, so a long value ("1,120 / 1,250 g") shrinks before the
  label does. Estimate at 402 pt wide: card inner ≈ 402 − 32 − 32 = 338, cell ≈ 160, label ~45, value up to ~100.
  Larger text sizes are the open risk for stage B.
