## 1. What changed, step by step

- **Scaffold** (`StepScaffold.tsx`): the frame's caption **"Step N of 9"** now sits over the
  progress rail (600 13 pt `text2`; the number is the quiz position the rail already encodes).
  The frame's back button and ghost "Skip" are not drawn — behavior the decisions do not grant
  (see Deviations).
- **1 Welcome** (`ValueHeroScreen.tsx`): the step leaves the scaffold and becomes the frame's dark
  stage — `ember` background in both schemes, a drawn plate (SVG rings + food dots in `success` /
  `accent` / ember-text alphas), four accent corner brackets (stroke 6) and the
  **"High confidence · 624 kcal"** badge on `successTint`/`successInk`. The badge holds a kcal
  number, so it is wrapped in the design system's `EdSafeCollapse` — no policy branch in the
  feature. Locked copy stays verbatim, including the three feature rows; the CTA keeps
  `onboarding.getStarted`. The plate is a drawn stand-in: the branch carries no image asset.
- **2 Goal**: no code change needed — the branch's `OptionCard` already draws the frame's icon
  tile + selected check (U-71), and the locked copy differs from the frame's words (Deviations).
- **3 About you**: unchanged. Height/weight stay `WheelField`s — the frame's typed 56 pt field is
  the batch-2 `FillField`, and building it inside the feature is forbidden. The DOB wheel, the
  under-18 gate and its verbatim footnote stay (behavior + identifiers the brief keeps).
- **4 Goal weight and pace** (`GoalWeightPaceScreen.tsx`): the clamped-pace floor banner left
  `BannerNote` (success green) for the frame's caution tokens — a feature-local caution surface on
  `cautionTint`/`caution`/`cautionInk` with the locked sentence rendered through the design
  system's `markdownSegments` (the bold lead stays bold). `onboarding.safetyFloor` is unchanged.
  The pace slider stays until the batch-2 `Segment` lands.
- **5 Activity and diet**: unchanged — the stored vocabulary (five levels, five diets) is locked
  by the decision, and the cards/chips already render through the Ember `OptionCard`/`ChipOption`.
- **6 Projection** (`ProjectionScreen.tsx`): the three gridlines now stand at real kilogram
  values (max / mid / min of the data, labeled to the right at 0.5 kg steps — the frame's
  "81 / 78.5 / 76" pattern), the shaded band is the frame's 35 % opacity, the curve is
  `accentStrong` at 3.5 px and the goal endpoint is hollow. The chart keeps `kg + weeks` only;
  the no-calorie-string test still passes. The SVG width is fixed at 320 so the labels align
  (the old `width="100%"` scaled unpredictably against absolutely-positioned labels).
- **7 Privacy, Health and ED-Safe** (`PrivacyHealthScreen.tsx` + model + route): the frame's
  ED-Safe row joins the settings group as a **switch** (`onboarding.edSafeToggle`, fat-colored
  tile and track). Contract = the Settings wellness toggle's exact order: the model persists
  `'1'`/`'0'` under the injected key **first**, then applies the design flag — a failed write is
  recorded (`safePolicyWriteError`) and rethrown, and the view's handler catches it, so the mode
  never applies on a failed write. The key literal and `setEdSafeMode` live in the route file
  (`app/onboarding/index.tsx`), the same placement as `app/settings.tsx`; the model takes
  `policyStorageKey` / `applyPolicyFlag` / `writeSetting` as injected dependencies. The switch's
  value reads the design system's live flag (`useEdSafeMode`, the reading the standing-order rule
  20 allows for a control's state), so the mode applies at once across every screen. Meal
  reminders are not drawn (no stored preference; follow-up).
- **8 Building your plan** (`GeneratingPlanScreen.tsx`): the percent readout, the 4-step checklist
  and the two-second window are gone. The step now shows the indeterminate ring (fixed stub arc,
  sparkles in the centre — no number in the middle) over the locked title, and the frame's formula
  card computed by `TargetsEngine` over the same draft: **"Resting energy (Mifflin-St Jeor)"** →
  `bmr()`; **"× <activity title> <factor>"** → `tdee()` (titles shared with step 5's `LEVELS`,
  factor from the exported `ACTIVITY_MULTIPLIER`); **"± Pace <engine pace> kg/week"** → the plan's
  real `|tdee − dailyKcal|` (only for lose/gain — the quiz carries no pace for maintain/habit);
  separator; **"Daily target"** → `dailyKcal` kcal in `accentInk`. The card collapses under
  ED-safe mode through `EdSafeCollapse`. Generation is synchronous, so the reveal lands at once —
  `beginGeneration` computes and pushes `planReveal`; the timer, `MINIMUM_GENERATION_SECONDS`,
  `GENERATION_STEPS`, `generationPercent`, `generationStates`, `dispose()` and the model's
  `disableAnimations` dependency are removed (the `--ccDisableAnimations` flag itself stays for
  the scan feature). The comma-grouping helper moved to `format.ts` (shared with the reveal).
- **9 Your plan** (`PlanRevealScreen.tsx`): the kcal hero sits on the design system's
  `EmberCard` (`emberText`/`emberText2` tokens), and `EdSafeCollapse` now wraps the whole card, so
  ED-safe mode collapses number and card together (previously a bare hero inside a plain page).
  `plan.kcalHero`, the a11y label and every other identifier are unchanged; the inline edit panel
  stays with `plan.adjust` and the `plan.edit*` identifiers, per the decision.

## 2. Needs a design component (screen · component · props)

1. `BodyMetricsScreen.tsx` · **`FillField`** (height) · `<FillField label="Height" value={draft.heightCm} unit="cm" keyboardType="number-pad" onValueChange={model.setHeightCm}>` inside the existing `onboarding.heightField` wrapper — the frame's typed 56 pt fill field.
2. `BodyMetricsScreen.tsx` · **`FillField`** (current weight) · same shape, `unit="kg"`, `onValueChange={model.setWeightKg}`, wrapper `onboarding.weightField`.
3. `GoalWeightPaceScreen.tsx` · **`FillField`** (goal weight) · `value={draft.goalWeightKg}`, `unit="kg"`, `onValueChange={model.setGoalWeightKg}`, wrapper `onboarding.goalWeightField`; the hero row follows the draft as today.
4. `GoalWeightPaceScreen.tsx` · **`Segment`** (pace) · `<Segment options={[Relaxed, Steady, Faster]} selected={draft.requestedPaceKgPerWeek} onSelect={model.setPace}>` — the engine clamps on write, so every stored value stays one of today's. The three concrete values need the owner's pick at the swap; my proposal is Relaxed 0.25 / Steady 0.45 / Faster 0.90 (inside today's slider range, engine-clamped). The plain-words pace card ("About 0.4 kg a week …") belongs to the segment's selected state and lands with it; `onboarding.paceSlider` is replaced by the segment then (recorded at A2).
5. `BodyMetricsScreen.tsx` · **`Segment`** (sex) · `<Segment options={[Female, Male]} selected={draft.sex} onSelect={model.selectSex}>` — "Prefer not to say" stays out (engine follow-up).
6. `GeneratingPlanScreen.tsx` · **`EdSafeNote`** · `<EdSafeNote label="Balancing your meals" />` — the design component that renders only while ED-safe mode is on (the inverse of `EdSafeCollapse`). Without it the frame's ED-safe variant of step 8 cannot be drawn without a policy branch in the feature; the test "under ED-safe only the one line" lands with the swap.
7. `StepScaffold.tsx` · **`GlassIconButton`** (nav back) · needs the owner's back-navigation decision first — the flow has no `back()` today (see Deviations).
8. `ActivityDietScreen.tsx` · **`ChipOption` height** (existing component, design-side tweak) · the frame draws 44 pt chips; `ChipOption` is 36 pt with no prop. Propose an optional `minHeight` prop.
9. `ActivityDietScreen.tsx` · **`OptionCard` selected background** (design-side tweak) · the frame tints the selected card; today `OptionCard` fills selected cards with `colors.card`. Propose an optional selected-tint prop.

## 6. Model/route notes for the owner

- `disableAnimations` left `OnboardingModelDeps` (nothing reads it — there is no window to skip);
  the route no longer passes it and every test factory dropped it. The debug flag itself stays in
  `launchArgs.ts` (the scan feature still reads it).
- `dispose()` is gone (no timers left to cancel) and the flow's unmount cleanup went with it.
- `safePolicyWriteError` is recorded on the model but not surfaced on screen — the decision does
  not name an error surface for step 7; Settings shows a banner for the same failure. If the
  owner wants parity of treatment, a banner at A2 is a one-line add.
- Stage-B note: the generating step is transient by design (the engine computes synchronously and
  the flow advances at once), so the OB-08 evidence row cannot show it through a plain flow walk —
  the capture plan needs the owner's call (e.g. a fixture pause or asserting the ED-safe row only).
