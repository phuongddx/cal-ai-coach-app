Every new picture (frames 29–107) was opened before its block was written; frames 01–13, 15–17,
19–28 were left as the first catalog has them, per the brief. Differences found:

1. **Frame 89 (`89-glance-live-activity-lock-screen.png`).** The canvas figure stacks two cards
   (processing + ready-to-review) and the first catalog describes both. The PNG holds only the
   processing card: the capture tool photographs `#glance figure.frame > :first-child`, and the
   ready card is the second child. The catalog block now describes the picture and says the ready
   card is in the canvas figure, not in the PNG (a unit comparing against the PNG alone would miss
   the ready state).
2. **Icon panels carry their captions.** Frames 92–102 are panels (no `.phone`), so their PNGs are
   the whole `figure.frame` including the figcaption text. Not a disagreement with the generator —
   just how panels capture; the blocks say what the picture shows.
3. Frames 105 and 106 draw the step label and the three-part bar exactly as the generator says
   ("Spotting the foods", "Finding each food", "Adding it up"); the first-round frame 89 showed a
   percentage-style bar — the catalog notes the newer frames replace that presentation.
