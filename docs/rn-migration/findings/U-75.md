## 1. What was built

**Progress (PG-01…PG-04).**

- The range segment: the design `Segment` in its `text` variant — Week / Month / 3M / Year, Month
  selected — with `progress.range` on the row and `progress.range.<value>` per option.
  `ProgressModel.weightPoints(range)` slices the same local series over the trailing window
  (`RANGE_WINDOW_DAYS`: 7 / 30 / 90 / 365 local days); without a range it returns the full series
  as before, so the ported twins are untouched.
- The Ember weight chart: `screens/WeightChart.tsx` — the `accent` area fill at 10% under an
  `accentStrong` 3 pt line, the integer-kg gridlines that fit the data with right-side labels
  (`weightAxisTicks`), the end dot, the first/last date labels ("27 Oct" style), the
  `chart-card` testID and the frame's accessibility sentence with the real numbers
  (`weightTrendA11yLabel`: "Weight trend from 82.4 to 80.6 kilograms over 30 days").
- The trend card follows the frame: "Weight trend" label, the big current value (28 pt), the
  direction row over the selected window ("−1.8 kg in 30 days" in `successInk` when lost,
  "+N kg in N days" otherwise, "holding steady" flat; still icon + text, never color-only), and
  the "Log weight" `accentTint` chip (44 pt) inside the `progress.logWeight` wrapper. The sheet,
  its wheel and `progress.saveWeight`/`progress.closeWeightSheet` are unchanged.
- Weekly energy: the frame's header row — "Weekly energy" + "avg 429 kcal" over the seed
  (`averageKcalText`, the same sum/count arithmetic the deficit footer used) — the seven bars in
  `accentStrong` with their value labels, today's bar on `fill` with a dashed border and its day
  initial in `accentInk`, and the dashed average line across the bar band. The "Avg deficit"
  footer is gone (the frame does not show it). `EdSafeCollapse` still wraps the whole card.
- Badges: one "Badges" grid — earned tiles first, then locked, Swift order — drawn as feature
  tiles (see the design gap below) that keep `badge-tile:<name>` and `badgeA11yLabel`; the glyphs
  are the Ember icon set (camera / flame / drop / target / trophy, `lock` when locked) instead of
  emoji (`badgeIcon`). The streak hero (`streak-card`) and the progress-photos row stay.

**Coach (CH-01).**

- The greeting buckets return plain strings — "Good morning" / "Good afternoon" / "Good evening",
  no emoji, no exclamation (frame 12) — rendered as the subtitle above the large "Coach" title.
- The weekly review is the Ember hero card: the "THIS WEEK" eyebrow in `emberAccent`, the sentence
  (the unchanged `encouragement`, habit framing), and the stat row — the design-system
  `WeeklyReviewStats`, so `weekly-review-stats`, `weekly-stat:<id>` and the ED-safe swap are
  untouched — mounted on a card inset inside the hero.
- The insights head is "Insights" (frame copy). Each card is a feature component with the
  `insight-card:<variant>` testID (hydration → `info`, the rest → `accent`) and **at most one
  action row**: "See the days" (`coach.seeDays.<kind>`, 44 pt, `accentInk` + chevron) opens the
  local day list (`coach.dayList`, "Mon, Jan 12" rows) of the days behind the insight. "Set a
  reminder" is not built and shows no row; no insight action needs an endpoint.
- The trust copy is verbatim and permanent.

**Where the day list's days come from.** The coach snapshot loader already reads the 28-day
logged-day set and the week's kcal rows; it now returns them (`loggedDays`, `weekDays`) and
`coachModel` derives each insight's days locally (`insightDaysFor`, pure, tested): the streak
insight lists the consecutive logged days ending today; the weekly-average kinds list the week's
logged days; a kind the snapshot holds no days for — hydration (water is not in the read yet) —
lists none and therefore shows no action row. No new endpoint, no new table read.

## 2. Needs a design component

The brief forbids editing `apps/mobile/src/design/**`; each gap below is drawn in the feature
today and belongs in the design system:

1. **An Ember chart** (area fill, fitted axis with labels, date labels): drawn in
   `features/progress/screens/WeightChart.tsx`; the `chart-card` testID rides on the feature root.
   The DS `ChartCard` keeps its old shape for any other caller.
2. **`BadgeTile` needs an `iconName` option** (the same additive prop `OptionCard` got in U-71b):
   the tiles are drawn in `AchievementsView`, keeping BadgeTile's testID and accessibility label.
3. **`InsightCard` needs an action-row slot and Ember icon names**: the cards are drawn in
   `CoachView`, keeping `insight-card:<variant>`.
4. **`WeeklyReviewStats` needs an `onEmber` styling** (the `CalorieRing` precedent): stage A
   mounts the DS component on a `card`-colored inset inside the `EmberCard`, because its
   `accentInk`/`text2` values are unreadable directly on ember in light mode.
5. **A token for the hero tile grey `#2A2724`** (no light-mode counterpart, the same class of gap
   U-71b recorded for the dark segment pill): the stats inset uses `colors.card`.

## 3. Deviations

1. The weekly energy stays the trailing seven days under every range — the frame shows seven bars
   with Month selected. The window applies to the weight-trend series; "the weight trend and the
   weekly energy read the same local series" is read as *the same local data, no new source*, not
   one shared window.
2. The "Avg deficit −1721 kcal/day" footer is removed; the frame's "avg N kcal" header figure
   replaces it. The kcal arithmetic is unchanged: the average is the same sum/count, `scaleKcal`
   is untouched, and today's bar still carries its value label and a11y label.
3. The over-target caution tint on a day bar is dropped — the frame draws every logged bar in
   `accentStrong`. Status stays non-color-only through the value labels and column labels.
4. The streak hero (`streak-card`) and the progress-photos row stay, although the frame's
   Progress tab shows neither: the identifiers must survive and the brief's decisions do not
   remove them.
5. The "All" link beside the frame's "Badges" head is not built — there is no badges-list screen
   to open, and a dead control would break rule 16.
6. The weight chart's iOS audio-graph descriptor is not reachable from React Native; the text
   summary (`weightTrendA11yLabel`) stays the accessibility answer, as the brief decides.
7. The insight icons are SF Symbols (`flame`, `drop.fill`, `fork.knife`), not the Ember glyphs —
   the InsightCard gap above.
8. The frame's insight glyphs (sunrise/drop) and its sample copy ("Breakfast is your lightest
   meal") are illustrative; the app renders its seeded insights (streak / hydration / protein)
   with the frame's layout.

## 7. Not verified

- No Simulator, Metro or native build (stage A holds no slot): the Ember look is asserted at the
  token/style level in Jest; the frames were read and followed, not pixel-compared. Screenshots
  belong to stage B.

  iOS-only in React Native (Android falls back to solid).

- The chart draws only under `onLayout` with a positive width; Jest exercises the accessibility
  label and the pure helpers, not the painted SVG.
