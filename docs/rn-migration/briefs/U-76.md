## Decisions already made

Each block answers the "Differences" and "Behavior" lines of one frame.

**The header and the week strip.**

- The header has the date as a subtitle and "Today" as the title, as the frame does. The calendar
  entry point is a glass button in the header (`GlassIconButton`, icon `cal`, the label "Open
  diary"): it carries `today.openDiary` and opens the diary. `today.dateSelector` stays on the
  date subtitle. Say in the findings what the date selector did before, if it was a control.
- The week strip has seven cells of 44 by 56 pt with the radius of the frame; the selected day
  takes the accent tint and the accent ink. `today.weekStrip` and `today.dayCell` stay.

**The ember card.**

- The ring sits on the ember card (`EmberCard`): a ring of 150 pt with a 13 pt stroke. `size.ringMd`
  and `size.ringMdStroke` exist in the tokens. `CalorieRing` has no variant for them yet: you may
  change `apps/mobile/src/design/components/CalorieRing.tsx` and its test, additively, for this one
  purpose (a size and a look that read on ember), and list the change in your findings. No other
  file under `apps/mobile/src/design` changes.
- The centre of the ring shows the number left and the words "kcal left": the label follows the
  frame, and the test that expects "remaining" changes the string it expects, never its sentence.
- The right column shows "Eaten" and "Goal" with the values. "Burned" is not drawn: it needs the
  steps of Apple Health, which the app does not read yet (a follow-up). `today.ring` stays on the
  card.
- **The four-digit number.** The number left must stay on one line inside the 150 pt ring, with
  four digits such as 2150. Jest cannot measure text: write in the findings how you made sure
  (the `fitWidth` prop of `ScaledNumber` exists), and prove it on the Simulator in stage B.

**The macros.**

- A grid of two by two: Protein, Carbs, Fat and Fiber. Labels and values never wrap, and no card
  is cut at the edge of the screen. The screenshot of U-79 shows the third macro card cut at the
  right edge on `main`: the grid replaces that row. `today.macroCard.<key>` or the identifiers of
  today stay; name what you keep.

**What goes.**

- The "Health Score" card and the "Steps" card are not in the frames and both show a dash today.
  They go, with `today.healthScore` and `today.stepsCard` recorded as removed, with the reason.
  They come back when a score engine and the steps exist.

**The water card.**

- `WaterCard` and `WaterLogSheet` live in the diary feature and belong to U-74, which restyles
  them on the frame of Today: litres in the normal mode ("1.25 of 2 L" and the chip "+ 250 ml"),
  glasses under ED-Safe ("5 of 8 glasses" and the chip "+ 1 glass"). You mount the card as it is
  and keep `today.waterCard`.

**The meals and the streak.**

- A section head "Meals" with a "Diary" link, the meal rows with their badge and their kcal, the
  streak card with the flame icon and its freeze line. `today.recentMeals`, `today.foodRow`,
  `today.streakCard` stay. The last meal clears the tab bar and the Scan button, as today.

**The offline state (frame 08).**

- A caution chip "Offline · syncs later" in the header, on the caution tokens, with `offline.badge`.
  A meal that waits for the sync carries the suffix "syncs later". The dark look is the one of the
  frame: the background true black, the cards the warm dark of the tokens.

**ED-Safe (frame 09).**

- The centre of the ring shows "On track" and "N of M meals". M is the number of meals the plan
  expects in a day and N the meals logged so far, both from data the model already holds. If the
  model holds no such number, the sentence is left out and you say so.
- The right text is a guidance sentence whose second part names the next meal of the day by the
  clock ("Dinner is next."). The copy of the frame is the model; derive the meal from the hour.
- The macro grid stays. The meal rows drop the kcal, as `FoodRow` already does. No red "over"
  state anywhere. The hidden kcal elements keep their identifiers on the collapsed wrappers.

**The shell.**

- `apps/mobile/src/app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx` is yours in this unit: the icons of the tab bar follow
  the design (Coach `sparkles`, Profile `person.crop.circle`; read the canvas for the other two).
  The floating Scan button takes `size.fab` (62).
