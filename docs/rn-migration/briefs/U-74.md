## The ED-Safe rule of the feature

`DiaryDayView.tsx` and `AddFoodSheet.tsx` call `useEdSafeHidden()` today. Rule 20 forbids that.
A calorie figure is hidden with `EdSafeCollapse` from `src/design`. A note that appears only under
ED-Safe is `EdSafeNote`, when it is on your branch. At the end,
`grep -rnE "useEdSafeHidden\(|edSafeHidden\(|edSafeMode\(" apps/mobile/src/features/diary` prints
nothing.
