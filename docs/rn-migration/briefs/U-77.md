## Decisions already made

Each block answers the "Behavior the frame needs" line of one frame.

**Profile.**

- The name is the account's: the display name the session carries when the sign-in gave one, else
  the e-mail address. Never a hard-coded name.
- The plan line reads the entitlement state that exists: "Free" today. "Plus · renews …" waits for
  the store: a follow-up.
- The three quick stats of the frame carry the identifiers of the Swift stats where the fact is the
  same: `profile.stat.dailyTarget`, `profile.stat.weight`; the goal weight gets
  `profile.stat.goalWeight`. `profile.stat.daysLogged` is recorded as removed.
- The Apple Health row shows the real connection status as its value. `profile.healthNote` stays
  for the footnote path.
- The kg / lb switch is not built: it is unit conversion across the app, a new feature. The row
  shows the unit and no chevron. Record the deviation.

**Settings.**

- No product exists, so nothing is for sale in this build. The subscription card shows the plan
  and the scan limit from the entitlement state. `settings.changePlan` and
  `settings.cancelSubscription` stay, visibly disabled, and `settings.billingNote` says that plans
  are not available in this build. "Billing history" and "Restore purchases" are not drawn.
- The ED-Safe subtitle keeps today's copy until the widgets and the Live Activity honor the mode
  (U-78). Record the deviation.
- The burned add-back small copy states its effect, as the frame does.
- **Export.** Opening Settings only prepares the file, as today. The tap on `settings.row.export`
  calls `exportCsvAndShare`: the share sheet with the file. Three states follow the frames:
  - the write failed: an error row under the export row with the reason and a retry;
  - no entries to export: an information row that says so;
  - the share was cancelled: it is not an error. An information row says "Not shared", the file
    stays, with "Share again" and "Done". Read the typings of the share result in `node_modules`
    before you detect the dismissal.
  New identifiers: `settings.exportError`, `settings.exportEmpty`, `settings.exportCancelled`,
  `settings.exportRetry`, `settings.exportDone`.

**Target edit sheet (PR-03).**

- It follows the four frames: light, dark, ED-Safe, and the one with a goal under the healthy
  floor. ED-Safe hides the calorie numbers and keeps the weight.
- The guardrail of the frame: a goal under the healthy minimum for the saved height is clamped to
  the lowest healthy weight, the copy never says "BMI", the colours are caution, and the button
  names the clamped value. Read `packages/core/src/targets.ts` first: every save goes through
  `TargetsEngine`, which you may not edit. If the engine has no floor for the goal weight, do not
  invent one: keep today's sheet logic on the tokens, do not draw the floor card, and list the
  engine change as a follow-up.

**Paywall and cancel flow (a new feature folder, `features/billing`).**

- The paywall is the shell of the frame: the option cards, Restore and Close always visible,
  nothing selected apart from the plan choice, the terms under the button. It has three states:
  loading, failed, and ready. In this build no store is connected, so it always ends in the failed
  state: "Prices could not be loaded", with Retry and Close. It never shows a sample price.
- The cancel flow is the informational screen of the frame. Its primary opens the subscriptions
  page of the App Store through `Linking`, because the in-app sheet needs a native module that does
  not exist yet (decision D14 of the spec).
- Two route files: `apps/mobile/src/app/plans.tsx` and `apps/mobile/src/app/cancel-flow.tsx`.
  Update `apps/mobile/src/__tests__/routes.test.tsx` for them.
- New identifiers, recorded in the identifier table: `plans.sheet`, `plans.close`,
  `plans.restore`, `plans.option.<id>`, `plans.primary`, `plans.state.<loading|failed|ready>`,
  `cancelFlow.screen`, `cancelFlow.keep`, `cancelFlow.continue`.
- The quota sheet of the scan flow is not yours: U-72 restyles it. In the stage A2 of U-72 its
  "See plans" opens `/plans`.
