## Decisions the owner took after `plan.md` was written

Cite these in the parts "Behavior the frame needs" and "Differences from today's screen".

- **Sign-in (61 to 67).** The frames draw a password. The app signs in with a code sent to the
  e-mail, and keeps that (parity AU-01). The entry step takes frames 61 and 62 as they are. The
  e-mail step takes the look of 63 to 67 with the code where the frame has a password. The segment
  "Sign in | Create account", "Forgot password?", the reset-link screen and the two notes under the
  dock are not drawn. The back button of the entry step is not drawn (the root gate puts nothing
  behind it). The privacy line "Meal photos are only used for your estimate, never to train models."
  and the footnote about Terms and Privacy Policy are not drawn until Phương supplies the promise
  and the pages.
- **The goal floor (71).** The app lowers a goal to the flat minimum the engine returns. The
  height-based minimum of the frame is an engine follow-up, and the copy never says "BMI".
- **Report a problem (75 to 77).** A report unlocks Save and stays on this device. The photo toggle
  is not drawn until an endpoint with storage exists.
- **The paywall (78 to 80).** A shell with three states and no price: it ends in the failed state
  until a store is connected.
