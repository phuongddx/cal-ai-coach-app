## Decisions already made

Each block answers the "Behavior the frame needs" line of one frame.

**Progress.**

- The range segment is the `Segment` in its `text` variant: Week, Month, 3M and Year, with Month
  selected. The weight trend and the weekly energy read the same local series over that window.
  Identifiers: `progress.range` on the segment and `progress.range.<value>` on each option.
- The weight chart takes the area fill, a vertical axis that fits the data with its labels, the
  end dot and the strokes in `accentStrong`. Its accessibility label reads as the frame does:
  "Weight trend from 82.4 to 80.6 kilograms over 30 days", with the real numbers.
- The audio graph of iOS is not reachable from React Native. The text descriptor stays the
  accessibility answer: record it as a deviation.
- The bar of today is dashed, its label takes the accent ink, and the average line is dashed.
- The badge tiles use the icons of the Ember set (camera, flame, lock) instead of emoji.

**Coach.**

- The greeting follows the time of day and has no emoji. The weekly review is the ember hero card:
  the eyebrow, the sentence and the three stat tiles, all from data the snapshot already holds.
- Every insight card has at most one action row.
  - "See the days" opens a local list of the days behind the insight, from the data the snapshot
    holds: a small sheet or an expanded list under the card. Identifier `coach.seeDays.<variant>`
    on the action and `coach.dayList` on the list. An insight whose snapshot holds no days shows
    no action row.
  - "Set a reminder" is not built: no stored preference exists, and nothing calls
    `refreshNotifications()` yet. That insight shows no action row. It is a follow-up.
  - An action that needs a new endpoint is not built: list it in the findings under follow-ups.
