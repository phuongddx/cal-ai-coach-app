## Decisions already made

Each block answers the "Behavior the frame needs" line of one frame.

**Viewfinder.**

- The mode row is the `Segment` in its `chips` variant with four options: Food, Barcode, Label,
  Describe. Describe is the text mode the model already has; it opens the describe sheet.
- The guidance lines are those of the frame.
- The "1.0×" zoom readout goes. It was a number the app invented. Record `scan.zoom` as removed. If
  the branch has a real zoom control, the identifier moves onto it: say which.
- The free-scan counter chip is not drawn. The server sends no limit before a scan: a follow-up.

**Analyzing.**

- The dwell timer and its checklist playback go. The screen shows the thumb with its ring and glow,
  "Reading your plate", "Usually under 5 seconds" and Cancel. It draws no checklist row and no
  escalation row, because the client observes no stage: the scan API answers in one request. When
  the server reports stages, the rows come back (a backend follow-up).
- Tests that assert the 0.5 second dwell, the watchdog playback or the timer are deleted and listed
  with this row as their reason. The tests of the model about cancel, stale work and quota routing
  stay.
- `scan.analyzing`, `scan.analyzing.thumb` and `scan.cancel` stay. `scan.checklist` and
  `scan.step.<step>` are recorded as removed, with the reason.

**Review.**

- `SheetHeader`: the back button is `scan.retake`, the title is "Review", the close button is
  `scan.close`.
- The header row: the 72 pt thumb, the editable title (`scan.titleField`) with the pencil glyph, and
  the confidence badge on its own row under the title (`scan.badge`): confidence only, no claim
  about the dish.
- The total card: "Total · AI estimate", the number (`scan.totalKcal`), the column of macro dots.
- The item the user touched last is expanded; on entering the review it is the first item. An
  expanded item shows its name, its kcal (`scan.itemKcal.<id>`), the 44 pt minus and plus buttons
  with the `GramsField` between them, and the `GramsRuler` under them. A collapsed item shows its
  name, its kcal line and the grams pill that expands it.
- The identifiers of the grams control stay where the tests and the harness find them:
  `scan.grams.<id>` on the wrapper, `scan.grams.<id>.increment` on plus, the decrement identifier
  the branch has today on minus, `scan.grams.<id>.field` on the field.
- Serving chips are not drawn. The scan contract carries no servings: a contract and backend
  follow-up.
- "Something wrong? Report it" is the relabelled entry of the correction sheet; its identifier
  `scan.fixIssue.<id>` stays.
- An unresolved item gets a ghost destructive "Remove item" (`scan.removeItem.<id>`): it removes the
  item from the review, and the total follows.
- A one-tap answer exists only for a candidate the server sent with the item. Read the contract
  (`packages/api-contract/src/contracts/`): if an unresolved item carries candidates with their own
  values, the card shows them as the frame does, and a tap resolves the item with that candidate.
  If it carries none, the card keeps the explainer, "Fix Issue" and "Remove item". Record which.
- The disabled Save states the blocker: "Resolve 1 item to save", with the count of unresolved
  items. `scan.save` stays.
- The meal segment is the `Segment` in its `text` variant. The default follows the time of day, as
  today. `scan.mealPicker` stays on the segment.
- The medium tier of the badge takes the caution tokens. The thresholds do not change.
- In ED-Safe the total card is replaced by an `EdSafeNote`, and the item rows show the grams pill
  only: no kcal, no macro. The badge, the segment and Save stay. The note reads "Numbers are hidden
  in ED-Safe mode." and "Grams stay, so you can still correct each item." The first line of the
  frame ("A balanced plate: protein, carbs and veg.") is a claim about the meal the app cannot
  verify: record the deviation.
- "Add a missing item" is built in stage A2, not now (it needs the food search as a picker, which
  the diary unit adds).

**Saved.**

- A full screen, not a toast that leaves after four seconds: the check circle, "Saved to Lunch"
  with the meal of the scan, the line with the dish and its kcal, the ember card "Today so far" with
  the number and its bar, the streak line with the flame icon, the Undo card ("Logged by mistake?",
  "Undo stays here until you leave", the 44 pt Undo chip), and the dock with the primary "Scan
  another" and the ghost "View diary".
- Undo stays until the user leaves. The tombstone flow exists; only the dismissal changes.
- The identifiers stay on the parts that remain: `scan.savedToast`, `scan.todaySoFar`,
  `scan.todayKcal`, `scan.streakCard`, `scan.addMore`, `scan.viewDiary`, `toast:undo` on the Undo
  chip. An identifier with no remaining part is recorded as removed.
- The kcal line drops under ED-Safe, as the design system already does for it.

**Quota, failed, describe.** The quota state and the failed state take the tokens; the quota sheet
itself belongs to U-77. The describe sheet is restyled on the tokens: it has no frame.
