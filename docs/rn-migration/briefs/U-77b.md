## Goal

Sign-in looks like its frames. What stays: the behavior (three ways to sign in: Apple, Google, a
code sent to the e-mail), the sentences of the thirteen tests, every identifier. Nothing shows a
claim the app has no source for.

## What the frames show and what the app does

The frames draw sign-in with an e-mail **and a password**: a segment "Sign in | Create account",
"Forgot password?", a reset link, a pause after five wrong tries.

The app signs in with a **code** sent to the e-mail (`/v1/auth/otp/request`,
`/v1/auth/otp/verify`), as the SwiftUI app did. The API has a password route for existing users
only (`/v1/auth/email`), no account creation by password and no reset route
(`apps/api/src/auth/routes.ts`).

**Decision: the behavior does not change.** The entry step takes frames 61 and 62 as they are. The
e-mail step takes the *look* of frames 63 to 67: the header, the title, the labeled field with its
focus ring, the docked button, the error under the field, the sentence that says what was sent,
with the code where the frame has a password. The segment, "Forgot password?" and the reset-link
screen are not drawn; they go in the findings under "Deviations" with this reason. A password
sign-in is a product decision for Phương and is not part of this unit.

## Decisions already made

**The entry step (frames 61 and 62).**

- Layout from top to bottom as the frame: the tile, the title, the subtitle, the privacy line,
  then at the bottom the three buttons with the "or" divider between Google and e-mail. The buttons
  sit above the home indicator; nothing is centered vertically any more.
- The back button of the frame is not drawn: the root gate makes `sign-in` the first screen of a
  signed-out session (`apps/mobile/src/app/_layout.tsx`), so nothing is behind it. Record it as a
  deviation.
- The tile: 64 by 64 pt with corners of 14 pt (`radius.btn`), the Ember ring icon. Make one asset
  from `docs/design/ember/icon/flat/AppIcon-light-1024.png` scaled to 192 by 192 px
  (`sips -Z 192 <in> --out <out>`), saved as `apps/mobile/assets/brand/ember-ring.png`. The same
  tile in dark: frame 62 shows the light icon in both schemes.
- Title "Sign in to keep your diary safe" (`typography.title`). Subtitle "Your meals, weight and
  streaks are backed up and stay in sync on your other devices."
- The privacy line, with the Ember icon `shield` in the accent ink: "We never post anywhere or read
  your contacts." Draw it after you checked that it holds: no contacts usage string in
  `apps/mobile/app.config.ts`, no privacy manifest entry for contacts, no code that posts. Say in
  the findings what you checked.
- "Meal photos are only used for your estimate, never to train models." (the `lock` line) is **not
  drawn.** The second half is a promise about the vision provider that the repository cannot
  prove; Phương decides it. It goes under "Deviations", placed so that adding the line later is a
  one-line change.
- "Sign in with Apple": the native `AppleAuthenticationButton` as today, `buttonType` SIGN_IN,
  corner radius `radius.btn`, style BLACK in the light scheme and WHITE in the dark scheme. Read
  the scheme from `useTokens()` (`isDark`), the source every component uses. Hidden when Apple
  sign-in is unavailable, as today. `auth.signInApple` stays.
- "Continue with Google": `SecondaryButton` with `bordered`, as today. `auth.signInGoogle` stays.
  The G mark of the frame is not drawn: the component has no leading icon and the icon set has no
  Google mark. Both go under "Needs a design component".
- "Continue with email": the frame draws a filled button with the `mail` icon. Use `SecondaryButton`
  without `bordered`, and list the leading icon under "Needs a design component". New identifier
  `auth.signInEmail`, the third entry point, named like the other two.
- The footnote "By continuing you agree to our Terms and Privacy Policy." is **not drawn**: the
  repository holds no Terms and no Privacy Policy page to link to. Record it under "Deviations"
  for Phương.
