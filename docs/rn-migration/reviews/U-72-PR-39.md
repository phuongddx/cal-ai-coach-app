Twin: 464, three presses on the increment of the first item, "508 kcal" (the half-up arithmetic shown in a comment). The sentences of the parity tests are unchanged; every changed or replaced sentence in the other two files matches the list of the findings.
2. THE UNDO OF A SAVE: a tombstone is written with a new op in one transaction and never cancels a pending op, so an acknowledgement in flight cannot resurrect the entry: the op of the tombstone follows and wins.
