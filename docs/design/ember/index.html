CoachCal · Design System v2 “Ember” · iPhone 390×844 · iOS 26

Neon orange, honest numbers.

One brand colour for the icon, app, widgets and web. Orange fills always carry black labels, and orange text uses a darker “ink” on light backgrounds. The scan loop keeps the confidence badge visible on every scan, lets you type grams directly, and keeps billing one tap from Settings. ED-Safe hides every calorie number, including the four sheets that used to show them.

0 · Design system

Light, dark and ED-Safe share one set of tokens. Neon orange #FF6A00 is only used as a fill (black label, 6.89:1) or as a graphic on dark. For text and lines on light backgrounds there are two darker tokens, accent-ink #B84300 (5.47:1) and accent-strong #E35A00 (3.67:1). Warning moves to yellow and carbs to teal, so orange always means the brand.

1 · Scan loop: Viewfinder → Analyzing → Review → Saved

The core of the app. It should take under 5 seconds from shutter to an editable estimate. The confidence badge sits on its own row so it never truncates. Grams can be typed, nudged with 44 pt steppers (the 3-tap 464→508 kcal test path still works), or set by dragging the ruler.

2 · Today, Diary, Progress, Coach

The tab bar uses the standard iOS 26 floating glass with no custom background. Scan is a separate accessory button next to it. Coach now uses a sparkle icon (was bell) and Profile a person (was gear).

3 · Profile, Settings and the ED-Safe leak fixes

Every row now does something. Rows with a value open a picker, and rows without an action lose their chevron. The subscription card has working Change plan and Cancel actions.

4 · Quota, Plans, Cancel: transparent billing

Every billing surface has a close button. Price and trial terms sit together (App Review 3.1.2(c)). Restore is one tap. Manual logging stays free and is always offered. Cancelling uses StoreKit's in-app manage-subscriptions sheet and asks no guilt-trip questions (5.6). All prices are placeholders. The owner still owns the paywall copy.

5 · Onboarding: 9 steps

Steps share one scaffold: a progress rail, a back button, a large title, and one pinned primary button. The plan step is honest and shows the real formula instead of a fake progress bar. ED-Safe can be chosen at step 7, before any number is shown.

6 · Widgets and Live Activity

The widget shows the brand ring for the first time. The Live Activity covers the compact, minimal, expanded and Lock Screen presentations. The ED-Safe flag still travels in the content state, so the ED-Safe variants show “On track” and no number.

7 · App icon: “Ember ring”

One idea: an open progress ring that also reads as a C, with an ember dot where the ring stops. It is built as two layers (background plus foreground) for Icon Composer, so Liquid Glass can add its own highlight. The layers are square, unmasked and opaque. Default, Dark, Clear and Tinted variants keep the same shapes.
